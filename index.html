Our Story

From one kitchen table to a craft you can taste.

Cocoa Artisan began with a single stone melanger in a rented kitchen and a question: why does most chocolate taste the same, no matter where the cacao grew? We started visiting farms, tasting fermentations, and roasting in tiny batches until each origin's character came through.

Today we still roast, conch, and temper everything ourselves, in batches small enough to taste the difference a single harvest makes. No fillers, no cocoa butter substitutes — just cacao, cane sugar, and time.

  • Direct-trade sourcing — we pay above market price, every harvest.
  • Small batches — never more than 40kg of chocolate at a time.
  • Three ingredients, always — cacao, cane sugar, and cacao butter.

The Craft

From pod to bar, nothing is rushed.

  1. 01

    Harvest & Ferment

    Pods are hand-picked ripe, cracked, and the beans fermented in wooden boxes for up to a week to develop flavor.

  2. 02

    Dry & Sort

    Sun-dried on raised beds, then hand-sorted bean by bean for size and quality before the long journey to our workshop.

  3. 03

    Roast & Conch

    Small-batch roasted to bring out each origin's character, then conched for up to 72 hours for a silky texture.

  4. 04

    Temper & Wrap

    Hand-tempered for a clean snap and glossy finish, then wrapped by our small team the same week it's made.
